The Director's Vision

I am Abdalla Maher — an AI Film Director, Documentary Filmmaker, and Multimodal Producer based in Taipei, Taiwan. Operating as a full-stack production studio, I direct prestige historical series, remastered documentaries, and high-converting commercial cinema across Arabic, English, and Traditional Chinese.

In real estate and B2B markets, I produce Pre-Sale Real Estate AI Digital Twins (AI 虛擬樣品屋) and corporate freight explainers in Taiwan, backed by hands-on experience in Australian real estate marketing and property market analysis—translating Sydney and capital-city housing data into compelling visual stories for international buyers.

Every production combines locked 4K character and architectural identity, 24fps frame-accurate lip-sync, surgical spectral audio repair, and broadcast-grade -14 LUFS sound design—delivering agency-caliber cinema without traditional location overhead.

Engineering Meets Cinema

Every project begins with rigorous research and a locked visual bible.
