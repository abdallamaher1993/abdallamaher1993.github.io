About

I am Abdalla Maher — an AI video producer, documentary filmmaker, and digital storyteller based in Taiwan. I specialize in crafting cinematic AI-generated content that bridges history, culture, and imagination.

With a background in narrative filmmaking and AI-powered production, I create content that transforms complex stories into visually stunning experiences — from ancient Egyptian epics to futuristic anime, from historical documentaries to branded AI advertisements.

My work spans six platforms and reaches audiences worldwide, combining cutting-edge AI tools with traditional storytelling craft to produce content that is both technically impressive and emotionally resonant.

Services
