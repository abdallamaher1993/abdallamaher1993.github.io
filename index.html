24fps Lip-Sync & Surgical Audio QC

Auditing every syllable at 24fps with F5-TTS spectral mel-inpainting and self-donor voice grafting for zero timbre shift.

  • Self-donor native voice grafting & F5-TTS mel-inpainting
  • Strict listener closed-mouth verification during off-screen speech
  • Pre-mix spectral quality gate (Top5Bin <= 8.5%, zero synth hiss)

Gate 05 Deliverables

  • Synchronized AR, EN & ZH-TW master deliveries
